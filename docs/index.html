Try these questions as a reader

The live app requires an assigned demo account. Request credentials from Matthew Xu. Sign in as the reader to try these questions; the screenshot walkthroughs remain available without sign-in.

  • “What is the Calgary parking policy?” — look for an answer with its source.
  • “Who is the highest-paid employee?” — expect an access denial for this reader.
  • “What is the company policy for bringing pet llamas into the Calgary office?” — expect abstention if no sufficient authorized evidence supports it.

Role screenshots were captured locally. Hosted sign-in, reader permissions, HR-reader access, and writer proposal/rejection have also been verified. Credentials are shared privately. Captured answers document individual runs; live results and availability may vary.
